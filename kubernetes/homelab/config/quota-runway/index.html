What the estimate means

Solid lines use Keeper’s observed remaining-quota percentages. Dashed lines assume each account continues at its own measured pace, multiplied by the slider. Rate uses observed consumption within quota cycles; resets and time spent blocked at zero are excluded. At least five minutes of usable observations are required. Large gaps stay blank. Data older than 15 minutes, or past its stated reset, is marked stale and cannot produce a forecast.

Each reset marker comes from the provider’s next reset time. No later reset dates are invented. Resetting to a full allowance is an estimate until a new observation confirms it. Unknown consumption rates show current readings and reset markers without a descending line. Both short and weekly limits can restrict access; read the other-limit status on each account.

Each line averages the remaining percentage for accounts from the same provider and plan. Comparing Claude and Codex does not imply equal token budgets. These are usage estimates, not guarantees: model mix, long contexts, tools, and account routing change consumption.
