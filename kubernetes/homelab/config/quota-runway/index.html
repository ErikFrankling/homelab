How these numbers work

Tokens are every request Keeper recorded from the proxy (input including cache reads, plus output). Keeper has recorded since the proxy started; “All time” starts at the first recorded day.

“What you can still spend” stacks every enabled account of one provider. In tokens, each account's remaining percentage is multiplied by the tokens 1% of that account bought in its latest cycle with at least 20% used (accounts without one use the typical rate of their plan). Cache reads dominate the token count, so treat it as an estimate. The pace is allowance used across all of the provider's accounts (disabled ones too: that demand moves to the others) per hour while any account had some left. The expected line drains the account that resets first, refills each account at its reset and repeats every window. Keeper keeps 30 days of quota history.

Weekly cycles use Keeper's per-cycle token totals. “Tokens per 1%” counts only the stretches where the reading dropped by one point, so tokens that never moved the reading do not inflate it.
